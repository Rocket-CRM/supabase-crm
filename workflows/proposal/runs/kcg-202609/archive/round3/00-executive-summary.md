## Executive summary

Most of KCG's customers buy through channels KCG doesn't own. KCG Rewards turns each of them into a member KCG knows, gives them reasons to come back, and brings the repeat purchase increasingly to KCG's own channels. The KCG team runs it from one admin portal, inside KCG's LINE Official Account, from go-live on 1 January 2027.

**Own the customer, whatever the channel (TOR 1.1, 1.8).** Members join in KCG's LINE OA with LINE and a verified mobile number. Facebook and email sign-in widen the door, and we recommend always requiring LINE, the channel marketing automation reaches best (§02). All ten TOR 7 channels earn into one purchase history: marketplace buyers claim with their order number; Modern Trade and Makro PRO receipts are read by AI and approved by the KCG team or, optionally, automatically; flagship and booth sales come from the POS or the Front Line staff app; Brand.com and Shopify orders earn with no claim step (§03). KCG's current LINE members arrive with their history and any points balance (§02).

**Give members a reason to return (TOR 1.3, 1.5, 1.7).** One point currency, earned anywhere and spent on any reward, with optional tickets for campaigns such as lucky draws; KCG sets the conditions, the rates, and public or personalised multipliers (§04). One catalogue holds e-coupons, merchandise, extra points and lucky-draw entries, each with its own audience, limits and tier price (§05). Tiers make the next purchase worth more (§06). The KCG team builds Welcome, Up-Tier and Birth Month coupons, spending missions, Friend Get Friend, surveys and more in the portal, with no development per campaign (TOR 5-P1, §07).

**Move every member to the next purchase (TOR 1.6, 1.9, 9.4).** Analyze shows who stopped where: 14 report dashboards grouped by domain, segments and RFM, and AI analysis in the portal or in Claude or ChatGPT, limited to what each admin may see (§08). Activate moves them on (§09). Rule-based journeys, in the core scope, wait, check what the member bought and how they responded, then send the next LINE or SMS message or benefit (§10). AI decisioning, offered under TOR 9.4, gives every member an expert marketer: within the goal, actions and guardrails KCG sets, it decides for each member whether to act, wait or skip (§11, §13). On our estimates across customers, brands that add this activation layer see over 50% higher engagement and next-purchase conversion than brands running a points programme alone.

**Bring repeat purchase to KCG's own channels (TOR 1.4, 1.10, 7.4, 7.10).** KCG's own channels can earn at a better rate, so each reward nudges the next purchase toward them (§04). Brand.com earns from day one once the site sends us each order; on Shopify, the plugin does this automatically. When KCG's Shopify store opens, Rocket's plugin brings the programme into it: members see their balance, spend points and LINE rewards at checkout, get their tier's checkout benefits such as free shipping and 5% off for Gold, and refer friends, on the balance they built on Shopee and in Modern Trade (§12).
