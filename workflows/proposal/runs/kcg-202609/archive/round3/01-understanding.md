## 01 · The KCG Rewards journey

The rest of this proposal follows one member from first sign-up to a repeat purchase on KCG's own channels, stage by stage; each section keeps the TOR heading and tags clauses inline, e.g. "(TOR 5.1.1)", so every requirement can be found where it is answered.

| Stage | What the member does | TOR clauses | Section |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Join** | Scans a QR code at the counter, event booth or on pack, or taps the KCG LINE OA rich menu; signs up in LINE with a phone OTP and gives consent | 3.4, 4.1 | 02 |
| **Earn** | Earns KCG Rewards Points on every channel: flagship and event POS, Modern Trade and Makro PRO receipts, six marketplace shops, the website and LINE OA | 4.2, 7.1–7.9 | 03, 04 |
| **Burn** | Redeems points for e-coupons, merchandise or lucky draw entries, and receives the privileges KCG pushes | 5.1.4, 5.1.5, 6.1–6.4 | 05 |
| **Grow** | Buys more, moves up a tier, and unlocks a better earn rate, tier-only rewards and tier-only campaigns | 4.3 | 06 |
| **Campaign** | Gets something new to do: welcome, up-tier and birth-month coupons, spending missions, friend-get-friend, surveys | 5.1.1–5.1.3, 5.2 | 07 |
| **Analyze** | Every purchase, redemption and tap lands on one profile; the KCG team sees who did what in dashboards, reports, Customer 360 and AI analysis | 1.9, 9.1–9.4 | 08 |
| **Activate** | Receives the next nudge at the right moment, in LINE or by SMS, from rule-based journeys or AI decisioning | 1.6, 9.4 | 09–11 |
| **Convert** | Buys again at the flagship store, on the website or on Shopify, with the same member profile and the same balance | 7.4, 7.10, 8.6 | 12 |

Section 13 brings the AI across Analyze and Activate together. Sections 14–16 cover services (TOR 10–13), why Rocket, and why our price looks low.
