## 09 · Activation: a journey doesn't move itself

Every feature in §02–07 is a stage a member can reach. None of them makes a member take the next step. Activation does: the right push, to one member, at the moment it will move them. It is where the programme's features become purchase frequency and lifetime value (TOR 1.3, 1.4).

### The journey KCG designs, and the one members take

On paper, every member walks the whole path: **Join** in KCG's LINE OA, **Earn** on every channel they buy through, **Burn** points on a reward, **Grow** into a higher tier, **Engage and return** through campaigns, and **Convert**: make the second purchase on the brand's own channels — Flagship Store, Brand.com, Shopify (§12).

In practice, most members stop somewhere.

| Where members stop | The push that moves them on |
|---|---|
| Joined at an event booth for the welcome gift, never claimed a receipt or order | Bonus points on the first claim, sent on LINE a few days after sign-up |
| Earns from Shopee and Lazada, never redeems | A reminder before points expire, showing a reward already within reach |
| Redeemed once, then quiet for two months | A win-back offer timed to that member — triple points on a purchase in the next 30 days |
| A few points short of the next tier as the period closes | Extra points on the purchases that close the gap |
| Never heard about this month's mission | A LINE message sent only to the members the mission suits |
| Buys only on marketplaces and in Modern Trade | A higher earn rate or a coupon on KCG's own channels |

Analyze (§08) shows who stopped where; activation moves each of them, automatically, the moment their data changes. That makes activation the fabric between every stage of the programme, and the place where personalised marketing and personalised campaigns actually reach each member (TOR 1-P1b, 1.6). A programme without it works for the members who would have come back anyway, and its results stay average. On our estimates across customers, brands that pair their programme with activation see over 50% higher member engagement and conversion to next purchase than brands that run the programme alone.

### Two ways to activate

Both run inside the same journeys, on LINE and SMS:

- **Rule-based marketing automation (§10).** The KCG team sets the trigger, the waits and the conditions; every member who matches gets the same treatment. It fits the moments KCG wants handled the same way every time: the welcome series, the birth-month coupon, the expiry reminder, the tier-up reward, the win-back after a set number of quiet weeks. Part of the core scope.
- **AI decisioning (§11, offered under TOR 9.4).** KCG sets the goal, the allowed actions and the limits; for each member, the AI decides whether to act now, wait or skip, and what to send. It handles the behaviour no set of rules anticipates — an expert marketer for every member, at mass-market scale.
