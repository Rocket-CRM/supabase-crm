    Phase 1 · Why these stages

    Why behavior + proximal first

    Missing environmental / disposition / distal in Phase 1 is intentional — those stages are not required to answer P1 questions.
    1 Baseline Light fix
    2 Context P2
    3 Disposition P2
    4 Behavior P1 focus
    5 Proximal P1 focus
    6 Distal P3

    P1 questions (answerable without full chain)

    • Who engages with which content topics? → retarget
    • Which events satisfy growers?
    • Who used which Syngenta products (survey + sparse labels)?
    • Can we bind event survey answers to members?
    • Does label upload even work for this audience? (pilot)

    Why missing stages are OK for now

    • Context / disposition matter for prediction & outbreak coordination — not for proving engagement mechanics.
    • Distal yield without product×season is a weak label — wait for spine.
    • Baseline already strong enough to segment invites; only patch irrigation / 2nd crop lightly.
    • Building LINE habit first beats asking abstract attitude questions to silent leads.

    Questions unlocked

    Principle: Who engaged, and what they did / used — observed behavior + thin product signals among LINE-reachable growers. Targeting & nurture, not prediction.

    Content & channel interest
    • Among LINE openers/clickers last 90 days, which topic clusters (pest ID, application, product education) get repeat engagement — and which crop×province cells over-index?
    Event experience
    • Of LINE-linked attendees, what share rate the event ≥4/5, and do first-time vs repeat growers differ by crop?
    Product exposure (thin) · Mechanic learning
    • Among linked product surveys (+ the small approved-label pilot), which SKUs / AIs show up most — concentrated in which provinces?
    • Of ~4k warm LINE invites to label upload, what % start and what % approve — enough to scale, or keep signals on surveys / field sales?

    Still cannot

    Blocked: Season / outbreak context, stated intent at scale, and farm outcomes. No causal or population claims yet.

    Environmental coordination
    • Which LINE rice growers in [province] report outbreak pressure this week at density worth a coordinated response?
    Intent & outcomes
    • What are this season’s grower objectives by crop (yield vs cost vs risk)?
    • Who will get better yield / be receptive to a new SKU if we push product X vs Y?

    Vacant P1: Broadcast to ~6.2k LINE → open 15–25% → click 20% of opens → ~200–500 tags (or ~600–1.2k if digital ~14k). Survey completes 8–12% of invited actives. Label: invite ~4k warm LINE · start 5% · approve 65% ≈ ~130–200 (cap <300).

    Principle: Conditional learning on a spine panel — product / exposure + context + reported farm outcome for the same grower. Directional insight with stated bias, not census.

    Conditional outcome learning
    • On the spine (~200–500 model-ready rows), under outbreak/season condition C, who reports better vs worse yield after product X — vs peers with similar baseline?
    Segment receptivity
    • Which engaged crop×province×objective segments look most receptive to a new SKU narrative — before a mass push?
    Sales narrative support
    • What attribution themes (product + condition → reported outcome) are strong enough for field talking points, with “panel, not population” caveats?

    Still cannot

    Blocked: Inferring the full CRM base. Exact economics stay field-sales depth.

    Population inference

    • Growing the panel without growing LINE (and spine) in parallel — P3 volumes stay capped by activation.

    Question-type principles by phase

    Phase 1 · Behavior / proximal
    • Can: engagement targeting, event quality, thin product exposure, mechanic tests
    • Cannot: outbreak coordination, intent at scale, yield / SKU prediction
    Phase 2 · Context / disposition
    • Can: seasonal risk sensing, objectives by crop, product×season panel, spend bands
    • Cannot: causal yield under conditions, 171k population claims
    Phase 3 · Distal on spine
    • Can: conditional outcome learning, segment receptivity, sales narrative themes
    • Cannot: census inference, exact kg/rai for the mass digital cohort
