# UK Property Tax Note — 13 Walton (~£7m)

**Status:** Company-owned, currently let residentially  
**Assumption:** England / Northern Ireland (SDLT). Scotland / Wales use different land taxes.

---

## 1) Individual buyer who wants to live in it

Two routes: buy the **property** from the company, or buy the **company** (shares).

### A) Buy the property (£7m)

Buyer pays **SDLT**. No ATED (individual ownership).

SDLT is charged **slice by slice** (not one flat rate on the whole price):

| | Main home only | Additional home |
|---|---|---|
| **SDLT on £7m** | **£753,750** | **£1,103,750** |
| Non-UK resident (+2%) | **£893,750** | **£1,243,750** |

**Rate bands**

Main home only:

| Slice of price | Rate |
|---|---|
| £0 – £125,000 | 0% |
| £125,001 – £250,000 | 2% |
| £250,001 – £925,000 | 5% |
| £925,001 – £1.5m | 10% |
| Above £1.5m | 12% |

Additional home (already owns another dwelling; no main-residence replacement):

| Slice of price | Rate |
|---|---|
| £0 – £125,000 | 5% |
| £125,001 – £250,000 | 7% |
| £250,001 – £925,000 | 10% |
| £925,001 – £1.5m | 15% |
| Above £1.5m | 17% |

First-time buyer relief does **not** apply at £7m.

**Company-structure point:** no special SDLT penalty just because the seller is a company. Practical restrictions are commercial/legal (board approval, lender consent, charges on title). Buying the property out is the cleaner route for owner-occupation.

### B) Buy the company (shares)

| Tax | Amount / effect |
|---|---|
| SDLT on the property | Usually **none** |
| Stamp / SDRT on shares | Typically **0.5%** of price paid for shares (e.g. £7m equity about **£35,000**) |
| ATED if they live in it | **£75,450 per year** (2026/27; £5m–£10m band) |
| ATED if company keeps letting commercially | Usually **£0 charge** (must still file) |

Living in a company-owned £7m home also often creates **benefit-in-kind / director’s loan** issues. For owner-occupation, buying the **property** is usually cleaner than buying the company and living in it.

**ATED bands (2026/27)** — relevant band for ~£7m is highlighted:

| Property value | Annual ATED |
|---|---|
| £500,001 – £1m | £4,600 |
| £1m – £2m | £9,450 |
| £2m – £5m | £32,200 |
| **£5m – £10m** | **£75,450** |
| £10m – £20m | £151,450 |
| Over £20m | £303,450 |

Charge is pro-rated for part-year occupation / ownership.

## 2) Empty more than 6 months while finding a buyer

**No UK national “vacancy tax.”**

**Council tax:** empty-home premiums are set by the **local council** (often from 1–2 years empty, not automatically at 6 months). Council tax itself is usually still due. Check the specific authority.

**ATED** (company still owns it, value ~£7m):

- While genuinely in a **rental business** (including a normal void / re-letting): usually rental relief = **£0 charge**.
- There is **no statutory 6-month ATED holiday**.
- **Practical reality:** a normal landlord void of about **3–6 months** while re-letting is commonly run without ATED applying.
- If you have clearly **stopped renting** to refurbish/sell (sale-led, not really re-letting): assume relief has ended and **£75,450/year** can accrue for those days.
- **2 years empty, no tenant, sale mode:** do not assume you are ATED-free.

Agent line “avoid ATED for 6 months”: **not a real rule**. At most it is a rough void tolerance while you still look like a landlord — weak if facts show sale-only.

**Who decides rental purpose?** You self-assess; HMRC can challenge. “Exclusively for a property rental business” is the test. Marketing mainly for sale while claiming you are “still trying to let” is usually weak.

**Filing:** tenant leaving does not mean file ATED the next day. ATED is annual. If relief truly ends mid-year, amend within about **90 days** of the change of circumstances.

## 3) Other UK tax areas to be aware of

| Area | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Corporation tax on sale (if company sells the property) | Company gain on disposal; often affects net proceeds / price |
| Share sale vs asset sale | Share sale: buyer usually pays ~0.5% stamp; buyer inherits company risks |
| ATED filing | If value > £500k, company normally files even when relief makes charge £0 |
| Income / corporation tax on rent | While let, rental profits taxed in the company |
| VAT | Usually N/A for residential; watch if any commercial angle |
| Non-resident rules | Extra SDLT (+2%); possible non-resident CGT issues depending on seller profile |
| BIK / extraction | If anyone occupies company-owned home personally |
| Next ATED revaluation | Values next reset **1 April 2027** (affects bands from later periods) |

## Bottom line

| Question | Answer at ~£7m |
|---|---|
| Individual buys to live in — property route | SDLT **~£754k** (only home) or **~£1.10m** (additional home); no ATED |
| Individual buys to live in — company route | ~**0.5%** on shares; if they live there, ATED **£75,450/yr** + likely BIK issues |
| Empty 6 months | No national vacancy tax; council premium depends on local rules; ATED only if rental purpose/relief has really ended |
| “6 months ATED-free” | Not reliable as a rule; fine as a short re-let void, not as a sale holiday |
